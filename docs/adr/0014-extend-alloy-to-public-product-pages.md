# Extend Alloy to public product pages

**Status:** accepted

Public Chassis Notes product pages use the same Alloy visual language as the authenticated workspace, with more editorial spacing and composition. A separate marketing skin would make real interface evidence feel disconnected from the product and split the brand into two visual systems. Public pages do not copy the authenticated application shell, and this decision does not redesign the separate sign-in and registration experience.

Public copy follows the canonical language in the [domain glossary](../../CONTEXT.md) and the product scope in the [landing-page brief](../chassis-notes-landing-page.md). Alloy's Commissioner prose, Fragment Mono technical values, accessible focus treatment, and supporting-text floor apply on both public and authenticated surfaces.
