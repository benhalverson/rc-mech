# Extend Alloy to public product pages

**Status:** accepted

Public Chassis Notes product pages and public authentication use the same Alloy visual language as the authenticated workspace, with focused compositions appropriate to each surface. Public authentication keeps its centered sign-in and registration flow, but does not copy the authenticated application shell. A separate marketing or authentication skin would make real interface evidence feel disconnected from the product and split the brand into multiple visual systems.

Public copy follows the canonical language in the [domain glossary](../../CONTEXT.md) and the product scope in the [landing-page brief](../chassis-notes-landing-page.md). Alloy's Commissioner prose, Fragment Mono technical values, accessible focus treatment, and supporting-text floor apply on both public and authenticated surfaces.
